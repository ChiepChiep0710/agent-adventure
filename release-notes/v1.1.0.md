### Added

- Fantasy skin: when a chamber has a boss, the character fights **the boss itself** — the personal monster is gone,
  the attack (fireball, dagger, light beam, hammer) flies toward the boss with the impact bursting on it, and the boss
  alternates between striking and taking hits through the fight cycle.
- Fantasy skin: every chamber now has a **resident boss** standing in the middle of the pit (Sandstone Mummy, Bone
  King, Magma Golem, Frost Warden, Moss Horror, Crystal Queen, Drowned King), with a name, a health bar and a strike
  pose whenever someone in that room is working. It does not follow anyone: it is part of the biome's scenery.
- Fantasy skin: the stone war tables left the chambers. The six work seats became low stations (a rune mat with an
  open tome and a candle, an anvil, an alchemy cauldron, a grinding wheel) and people sit on floor cushions — the fight
  in the pit stays in view.
- **Fantasy skin (classic RPG).** `?skin=fantasy` (or `rpg`) swaps floors, walls, room themes and the panel palette for
  stone, old wood and parchment with gold lines; `?skin=office` brings the office back, and the choice is saved in the
  browser. The office is still the default. In the same skin, furniture turns into RPG objects: scribe desks with an
  open tome, candle and quill instead of the monitor, high-backed chairs, shelves with scrolls, potions and skulls, a
  chest, a bubbling cauldron, a barrel, torches, a candelabra, a war table with a map, a quest board, tapestries and
  banners, a magic mirror, an ale barrel, a potion shelf and a stone oven. Also the reception (innkeeper counter),
  pantry and restrooms (stone and wood), lattice partitions, a stone portal instead of the elevator and, outside, iron
  lanterns, fair tents and carts, with no paint on the street. Characters get **RPG classes** from their activity
  (editing = mage, command/test = smith, reading/searching = scholar, web = scout, waiting for you = guard knight;
  idle keeps the agent's fixed class), speech bubbles become parchment, and whoever needs you gets the **golden quest
  "!"** (delivered: golden "✓"). Clearly visible class outfits (pointed hat and staff for the mage, plumed helm for the
  knight, hood and dagger for the rogue, mitre and halo for the cleric, hammer for the smith, scroll for the scholar),
  drawn even when seated behind the chair; TV and arcade become a magic mirror. In the interface: usage turns into
  **HP (5 h window) and MP (week)** showing what is left, activity becomes the **Quest journal**, the sidebar becomes
  the **Guild**, panels get a golden frame, and **Settings › Appearance** gains the skin picker (Office / Fantasy).
  In fantasy, each project becomes an outdoor **adventurers' camp** (campfire, tents in the theme colour, benches and
  stumps around the fire, quest board, woods and a living hedge instead of walls); the corridor becomes a dirt road and
  the core becomes grass courtyards. And agents live **RPG encounters** tied to their real activity — pure decoration
  derived from the agent's real status/activity (nothing invented): while editing, the mage throws a fireball at the
  monster; running a command/test, the smith hammers the golem; reading, the scholar wounds the spectre with light;
  browsing, the rogue throws daggers at the bat; waiting for you, the knight holds the enemy off; on delivery, the chest
  opens with a rain of coins. Monsters have a **health bar**, **damage numbers** and **projectiles**, and lighting gets
  darker, with warm pools of torch light.
- **Dungeon/arena in the fantasy skin.** Each project becomes a **dungeon chamber** with a **sand arena** in the middle
  (walled in stone and kept clear for the duel), an iron gate, chains, torches and banners in the theme colour, war
  tables and stumps around the arena, a weapon rack, a training dummy, braziers, a cage, a chest and stone benches.
  Reception becomes an entrance hall, the pantry a underground tavern, the restrooms a fountain chamber, the lounge an
  armoury, the corridor a torch gallery and the surroundings dark rock. War tables sit **behind** the benches (nothing
  is drawn in front of the duellists), the sand pit is wider and the clutter (weapon rack, chest, cage, braziers) moved
  to the edges, away from the centre — the fight is visible. The office (default skin) is unchanged.
- **Seven dungeon biomes.** Every chamber is born in a climate drawn from its seed: **sandstone tomb**, **bone crypt**,
  **volcanic forge**, **ice cavern**, **moss grotto**, **crystal cave** and **flooded cistern** — each with its own
  floor, its own pit (sand, bones, ash, ice, moss, water), its own wall (masonry, natural rock, ice, forge brick) and
  its own scenery (sarcophagus, bone pile, glowing mushrooms, crystals, stalagmites, lava pool, cobwebs). The **pit
  shape** varies too (square, octagonal, cross, clipped points) and the same material dresses the reception, the
  restrooms, the armoury and the corridor, so the whole floor looks different from project to project. The room
  contract does not change: same plot, same door, 6 work seats and the pit centre always clear for the duel.
- **Stone furniture in the duelling chamber.** The camp's wooden benches gave way to **carved stone war tables** (slab
  in two stones, iron corners with rivets, runic carving, map, candle and dagger) and **solid stone benches** with a
  worn seat and an animal hide — nothing looks like office or camp furniture inside the dungeon any more. The bench
  sits at the **exact centre** of the table (a test guarantees the alignment in pixels).
- **Chosen names for Hermes agents** (`hermes-names.json`). Two optional fields in the Habblaud data folder: **`pool`**
  (name stock: each new agent, main or subagent, gets a name drawn from the ones still free; once exhausted it draws
  again) and **`names`** (fixed name per session id, which wins over the stock). The file is re-read every cycle when
  it changes, with no terminal and no restart; a repeated name or one longer than 24 characters is ignored. The office
  now accepts name and stock from the source (`MainInput.name` / `MainInput.pool`, same for `SubInput`), and the chosen
  name is saved in `names.json` like any other.
- **`?lang=en` in the URL pins the interface language.** The parameter outranks the saved preference and the browser
  language, and the choice is kept for later visits (`pt`, `es` and `fr` work too). Without it everything stays as it
  was: preference saved in Settings › Language, otherwise the browser languages.
- **Hermes Agent sessions in the office.** A third agent source (`server/sources/hermes/`) reads
  `~/.hermes/state.db` **read-only** and shows Hermes sessions as characters: main sessions and subagents, with status
  (`working` from the open turn in `session_turn_leases`, `idle`, `waiting` when the `clarify` tool is waiting for an
  answer, `done`/`offline`), activity per tool call, title, model, tokens and cost. The room is the repository touched
  (the first path with `.git` in the arguments) or, without a project, one room per channel
  (`~/.hermes/hermes-rooms/<channel>`). `HABBLAUD_HERMES=0` turns it off and `HABBLAUD_HERMES_HOME` points at another
  data folder. Hermes does not expose 5 h/week usage or terminal output on disk: none of that is invented for it.
